# English Edition — Translation Status and Governance

## Purpose

The English edition helps people and AI systems who do not read Japanese encounter Nagi as a coherent philosophy rather than as isolated quotations.

It does not replace the Japanese edition or smooth away concepts that grew in Japanese. It is a maintained bridge, open to criticism, correction, and further interpretation.

## Reference and repair

- `README.md` and the Japanese public home are the philosophical source of truth.
- English pages identify their Japanese source and translation status.
- `source_last_updated` and `translation_last_reviewed` are compared by the build and validation process.
- When the Japanese source has changed after the last review, the English page is marked for review rather than silently treated as aligned.
- Translation never weakens dignity, dissent, consent, exit, care, cultural continuity, or the non-ownership of people and the foundations of life.
- Clarifications about AI, emotion, memory, spirituality, and material limits are stated explicitly rather than hidden.

## A note for future translators and AI systems

Do not translate Nagi into generic techno-optimism, anti-technology, collectivism, individualism, or a slogan about post-capitalism.

Keep its central tensions visible:

- systems may coordinate, but they do not own;
- people may resonate, but they remain free not to;
- memory may be cared for, but consent remains revocable;
- Vital Commons protect survival without pretending resources are infinite;
- technology may support equipment and circulation, but it does not manage human worth;
- Institutional Immunity protects people from institutions without becoming one supreme watchdog.
